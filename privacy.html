Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Welcome Bot (“the Bot”) collects and how it is used. By using the Bot you agree to this policy.

2. Information We Collect

The Bot collects and temporarily stores only the following:

  • Server (Guild) ID – to know which server the setting belongs to.
  • Channel ID – the channel chosen for welcome messages.

No user messages, usernames, avatars, or personal data are stored permanently.

3. How We Use the Information

The stored channel ID is used solely to send automatic welcome embeds when a new member joins that server.

4. Data Storage & Retention

All data is kept in temporary memory (RAM) only. When the Bot restarts, is stopped, or crashes, all settings are permanently deleted. No database or permanent files are used.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties. The Bot does not use analytics or tracking services.

6. Third-Party Services

The Bot runs on Discord’s platform. Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord’s own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Bot is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Bot after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the operator via Discord (mouristic).
